Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

How is Chavruta.AI different from ChatGPT?

Chavruta.AI is built specifically for Torah learning. Instead of relying on a general model's memory, it retrieves authentic Jewish texts from a curated Sefaria-based corpus and connects its answers directly to the underlying sources you can open and check.

Is it really free?

Yes. There is a generous free daily plan that lets you learn, explore sources, and build shiurim without entering any credit card information.

Does this replace a Rabbi or Halachic authority?

No. Chavruta.AI is an educational research and study assistant. It does not provide halacha l'ma'aseh (binding halachic rulings). Always consult a qualified Rav for practical halachic questions.

Does it handle Gemara Aramaic?

Yes. Sources from the Talmud are retrieved and quoted in their original Aramaic and Hebrew, accompanied by clear explanations in English (or Hebrew).

Who is this service intended for?

Chavruta.AI is designed for Jewish adults (ages 18+) who learn Torah regularly — whether studying daf yomi, preparing a shiur, exploring a sugya, or reviewing mefarshim.

What texts are covered in the corpus?

Tanakh, Mishnah, Bavli, Yerushalmi, major Midrashim, classic Rishonim (Rashi, Tosafot, Rambam, Ramban, Rashba, etc.), and foundational Poskim (Tur, Shulchan Aruch, Mishnah Berurah).
