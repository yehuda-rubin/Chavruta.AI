# Detailed Grading Rubric — Bagrut in Gemara Iyun (Exam 6281, 5 Units)
## Tractate & Sugya: [Tractate and Sugya Name]

### Grading Principles for Rishonim Iyun (Exam 6281)
1. **Precision in Tosafot's Structure:** Full credit on a Tosafot *kushya* requires stating: (a) the baseline assumption; (b) the conflicting source; and (c) why the two sources contradict.
2. **Explaining the Distinction (Chiluk):** Simply quoting "here it deals with X, there with Y" is insufficient; the student must explain the **halachic rationale (*svara*)** why the ruling differs between the two cases.
