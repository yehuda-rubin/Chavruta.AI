## 1. The 4-Step Method for Full Credit on a Bagrut Case Study (*Eru'on*)
1. **Highlight the Case Facts:** Who is acting? What were the exact circumstances (inadvertent/intentional, *lechatchila*/*bedi'avad*)? What question is asked at the end of the story?
2. **Locate the Matching Source (`[S#]`):** Identify the clause in the Mishnah, Shulchan Arukh, or Mishnah Berurah addressing that exact factual setup.
3. **State the Ruling & Rationale:** Write the explicit ruling (permitted/prohibited, liable/exempt) + the halachic reason from the source.
4. **Close the Loop (Apply to the Story):** "In our scenario, since [Character Name] did [Action], according to [Source `[S#]`] the ruling is...".
