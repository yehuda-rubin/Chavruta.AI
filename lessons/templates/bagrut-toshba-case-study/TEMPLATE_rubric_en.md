# Detailed Grading Rubric — Bagrut in Toshba: Educational Halachic Case Studies
## Unit Topic: [Halachic Topic]

> **Mandatory Advisory Note (Principle VIII):** This rubric is strictly for grading academic Bagrut exam responses based on the retrieved sources (`[S#]`) and never constitutes a practical halachic ruling (*psak*).

### Case-Study Grading Formula
1. **Accurate Ruling for the Scenario (30%):** Explicit verdict (permitted/prohibited, liable/exempt) tailored to the case facts.
2. **Source Citation & Halachic Rationale (40%):** Quote or reference to `[S#]` and explanation of the legal reasoning.
3. **Explicit Application to Case Facts (30%):** Connecting the characters, actions, and circumstances in the story to the rule in the source. *Copying the source without applying it to the story earns at most 60% of the item's points.*
