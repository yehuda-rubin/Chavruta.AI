## Common Student Pitfalls
1. **Merely Naming Literary Devices:** Identifying a "rhetorical question" or "parallelism" without explaining its implied meaning and rhetorical impact.
2. **Paraphrasing Instead of Analyzing:** Restating the verse literally instead of extracting the underlying theological, moral, or historiographical concept.
