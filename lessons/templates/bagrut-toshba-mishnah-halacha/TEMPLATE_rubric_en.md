# Detailed Grading Rubric — Bagrut in Toshba (Mishnah & Halacha, 3–5 Units)
## Unit Topic: [Tractate / Halachic Topic]

### Mandatory Toshba Grading Standards
1. **Strict Separation of Case, Ruling, and Rationale:** Conflating the factual scenario with the legal ruling deducts 50% of the item's points.
2. **Halachic Progression:** Answers must explicitly state both **which Tanna is followed** and **what conditions or clarifications are added** in Rambam, Shulchan Arukh, or Mishnah Berurah compared to the Mishnah.
3. **Principle VIII (Halachic Humility):** All rubric answers present academic analysis of the sources for Bagrut exam study only, never practical halachic rulings (*psak*).
