## Common Student Pitfalls (For Teacher & Automated Check Feedback)
1. **Conflating Peshat and Drash:** Attributing Rashi's specific halachic limitation (`[S2]`) directly to the literal wording of the verse (`[S1]`).
2. **Summarizing the Commentator Instead of Naming the Difficulty:** Failing to state the anomaly in the verse that triggered the commentary.
3. **Unstructured Comparison:** Describing each passage separately in Part III without aligning them along explicit comparative criteria.
