## Part II: Halachic Disputes & Classification of the Oral Law *(35 Points)*

### Question 2 *(35 Points)*

#### Sub-question 2a — Distinguishing Categories of Oral Law (`[S3]`, `[S4]`) *(15 Points)*
1. **Category 1 (Received Explanations / Halacha LeMoshe MiSinai) *(7.5 pts)*:** Laws transmitted directly from Sinai that are never subject to dispute + grounding in `[S3]`.
2. **Category 2 (Laws Derived via 13 Hermeneutical Rules / Rabbinic Decrees & Enactments) *(7.5 pts)*:** Laws derived through logical reasoning or enacted by the Sages, where differing analytical perspectives produce legitimate dispute + grounding in `[S3]`/`[S4]`.

#### Sub-question 2b — Causes of Dispute and Decision Rules (`[S3]`, `[S4]`) *(12 Points)*
1. **Factors Behind Proliferation of Disputes *(6 pts)*:** [Per source: differences in analytical reasoning (Rambam) / or insufficient discipleship during times of crisis ("when the disciples of Shammai and Hillel multiplied...") + quote].
2. **Decision Rule *(6 pts)*:** Majority rule (*"Follow the majority"*) and established canons of halachic decision-making.

#### Sub-question 2c — Protective Decrees (Gezeirot) vs. Enactments (Takkanot) *(8 Points)*
- **Gezeirah ("Protective Fence") *(4 pts)*:** A rabbinic prohibition enacted to prevent inadvertent violation of a biblical prohibition.
- **Takkanah *(4 pts)*:** A positive rabbinic enactment instituted for social welfare (*Tikkun Olam*) or religious enhancement.

## Common Student Pitfalls
1. **Chronological Confusion:** Confusing the era/redactors of the Mishnah (Tannaim, Rabbi Yehudah HaNasi, c. 200 CE) with the Talmud (Amoraim, Rav Ashi & Ravina, c. 500 CE).
2. **Conflating Mishnah and Baraita:** Failing to note that a Baraita is an "external" Tannaitic teaching not included in Rabbi Yehudah HaNasi's Mishnah.
