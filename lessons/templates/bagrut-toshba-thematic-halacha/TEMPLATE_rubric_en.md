## Common Student Pitfalls in Toshba
1. **Ruling Without Rationale ("Permitted/Forbidden" Only):** Writing a one-word verdict in a case study without explaining the underlying halachic reason and naming the authority (`[S4]`/`[S5]`) earns less than 50%.
2. **Confusing Mechaber and Rema:** Failing to distinguish between the base text of the Shulchan Arukh and Rema's gloss (*Hagahah*).
